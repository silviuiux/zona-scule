# Kärcher Master Nomenclature Blueprint & Master Catalog Guide
## Technical Reference Document for Authorized Suppliers

## 1. The Core Brand Distinction: Visual and Engineering Demarcation

Before reading any alphanumeric model codes, Kärcher equipment is immediately split into two distinct target demographics based entirely on structural chassis color-coding. This distinction represents an entirely different set of internal engineering tolerances, material compositions, and operational duty cycle ratings.

### Bright Yellow: Home & Garden Line
* **Target Market:** Private residential consumers and light domestic cleaning tasks.
* **Engineering Core:** Engineered for low to medium intermittent workloads. Components frequently utilize high-grade, impact-resistant composites, lightweight aluminum pump bodies (on mid-tier models), and standard air-cooled universal or light induction motors. 
* **Commercial Use Warning:** Utilizing yellow-line consumer products in commercial, industrial, or municipal environments typically voids the standard manufacturer warranty.

### Anthracite Gray: Professional Line
* **Target Market:** Industrial facilities, construction environments, commercial cleaning contractors, automotive repair shops, agriculture, and municipal infrastructure.
* **Engineering Core:** Built to endure multi-hour, continuous, daily duty cycles under punishing ambient conditions. Internal architecture relies heavily on high-end raw materials: solid forged brass pump cylinder heads, solid ceramic-sleeved pistons, stainless steel flow lances, and heavy-duty, oil-cooled or water-cooled low-speed 4-pole induction motors.

## 2. Product Family Categories (The Prefix Codes)

The leading letters of any Kärcher model code represent the specific product family or structural configuration platform. These are derived mostly from technical German functional descriptions.

### Professional (Gray Line) Prefixes
* **HD (*Hochdruck*):** High-Pressure Cleaners (Cold Water). Standard heavy-duty high-pressure sprayers for removing loose surface grime, mud, and structural grease.
* **HDS (*Hochdruck Dampf/Heißwasser*):** High-Pressure Steam/Hot Water Cleaners. Incorporate an internal oil-fired diesel burner coil, gas burner, or electric heating chamber to superheat water up to $155^\circ	ext{C}$ for intense grease, oil, and tar emulsification.
* **NT (*Nass / Trocken*):** Wet & Dry Vacuum Cleaners. Built with bypass-cooled suction turbines and liquid shut-off mechanisms to process heavy volumes of dry particulate matter and water/slurry concurrently.
* **T (*Trockensauger*):** Dry-Only Canister Vacuums. Designed primarily for the commercial hospitality, office, and contract cleaning markets where quiet operation and fine dust filtration on carpets or hard surfaces are paramount.
* **CV (*Carpet Vacuum*):** Commercial Upright Brush Vacuums. Feature a dedicated dual-motor design (one for suction, one to drive an active mechanical roller brush) to lift carpet pile and extract deep-set dry soil.
* **Puzzi:** Spray-Extraction Deep Cleaners (Carpet & Upholstery). Derived from the German word for "to clean" (*putzen*). These injection-extraction machines pressure-inject a surfactant solution deep into textiles and instantly extract the suspended soil via a high-airflow vacuum shoe.
* **SG / SGV (*Dampferzeuger / Dampfsauger*):** Commercial Steam Cleaners / Steam Vacuums. Sanitization units that output dry or wet continuous pressurized steam without high physical water volume impact. SG units are steam-only; SGV units feature an integrated extraction turbine to pull dirty liquid residue immediately away from the surface.
* **BR (*Brush Roller*):** Scrubber Driers with Cylindrical Roller Brushes. Engineered for textured or profiled floors, tile grout lines, and heavy-debris environments where the high-RPM counter-rotating rollers simultaneously sweep up loose grit while washing.
* **BD (*Brush Disc*):** Scrubber Driers with Flat Disc Brushes. Optimized for smooth, flat, or polished commercial floors. Provide high brush-on-floor contact time and are ideal for surface stripping, polishing, and standard daily washing.
* **B (*Bodenreinigungsmaschinen*):** Mid-to-Large Scale Scrubber Drier Platforms. Represents fully configured walk-behind or ride-on floor scrubber systems housing large solution and recovery tanks.
* **KM (*Kehrmaschine*):** Commercial & Industrial Sweeping Machines. Ranging from manual push-sweepers to fully enclosed industrial ride-ons, utilizing mechanical side and main roller brooms alongside heavy-duty vacuum dust filtration containment.

### Consumer (Yellow Line) Prefixes
* **K (*Konsumenten*):** Home & Garden High-Pressure Washers (categorized into tiers from K 2 to K 7).
* **WD (*Wet / Dry*):** Consumer-grade Wet and Dry vacuums for garages, workshops, and general domestic DIY use.
* **SC (*Steam Cleaner*):** Domestic steam mops and household surface sanitization canisters.
* **WV (*Window Vac*):** Handheld, battery-powered liquid suction squeegees for household glass, tile, and mirror maintenance.
* **FC (*Floor Cleaner*):** Residential hard-floor electric mops utilizing self-cleaning micro-fiber rollers to wash and vacuum floors simultaneously.

## 3. Decoding the Numeric Systems

The numeric sequences immediately trailing the primary family prefix follow a rigid mechanical formula depending on the machine class.

### High-Pressure Washers (HD / HDS)
Professional high-pressure systems prioritize a **"Flow Rate / Operating Pressure"** numbering matrix:
* **The First Digits (Before the Slash):** Specifies the maximum water flow volume capacity calculated in *hundreds of liters per hour*. For example, an HD **7** indicates approximately 700 liters per hour (l/h) flow rate. An HD **10** denotes 1,000 l/h.
* **The Second Digits (After the Slash):** Indicates the maximum working pressure rating measured in *tens of bar* (or total Megapascals, MPa). For example, a **/15** signifies a 150 bar (15 MPa) operating capability. A **/21** signifies 210 bar (21 MPa).
* *Example Application:* **HD 10/21-4 S** ➔ Cold water unit, delivers 1,000 l/h water volume, runs at 210 bar working pressure, driven by a premium 4-pole electric motor, belonging to the heavy-duty Super Class platform.

### Vacuum Cleaners (NT / T) & Sweepers (KM)
Vacuums and sweepers use a **"Chassis Capacity / Turbine Count"** or **"Structural Path Width"** format:
* **The First Number Group:** Denotes either the recovery tank/container volume capacity in liters (for vacuums) or the total structural sweeping path width in centimeters including the side brooms (for sweepers). For example, an NT **40** has a 40-liter waste container. A KM **70** has a 70 cm sweeping track width.
* **The Second Number Group (After the Slash):** Indicates the absolute number of individual electric suction turbines or drive motors installed inside the unit housing. 
* *Example Application:* **NT 40/1** ➔ Professional Wet/Dry vacuum platform, constructed with a 40-liter fluid container shell, powered by a single high-output suction motor.

### Scrubber Driers (B Platform)
* **Core Integer:** Signifies the absolute fresh water chemical solution tank volume capacity measured in liters.
* *Example Application:* **B 150 R** ➔ Heavy-duty industrial floor scrubber carrying a 150-liter solution tank capacity, configured as a Ride-on (R) chassis configuration.

## 4. Feature Modifiers & Suffixes (The Fine Print)

The letters, designations, and trade names appended to the tail end of the model string declare the exact internal technological sub-systems, safety ratings, or unique target workspace adaptations.

### Filter & Tool Integration Technologies
* **Tact:** *Triggered Air Draft Cleaning Technology*. This indicates Kärcher's premium, fully automated, sensor-governed filter cleaning framework. Every 15 to 60 seconds (depending on settings), the machine automatically triggers sudden bursts of pressurized air in reverse through the flat pleated filter element. This forcefully blows microscopic concrete, wood, masonry, or flour dust out of the pleats mid-operation, maintaining maximum airflow and suction without manual intervention.
* **Ap:** *ApClean*. Signifies Kärcher's mid-tier semi-automatic filter cleaning system. The operator manually depresses a mechanical blast button on the machine head while sealing the hose intake to clear the filter element of light dust accumulation via reverse atmospheric pressure.
* **Te:** *Tool Electronic / Telecontrol*. Denotes an integrated structural electrical socket on the machine body. This allows industrial power tools (wall chasers, grinders, orbital sanders) to be plugged directly into the vacuum. The vacuum automatically activates and deactivates in perfect synchronicity with the power tool's trigger assembly.

### Safety Dust Class Certifications (Legal Workspace Compliance)
* **L (*Low Risk*):** Certified for separating general ambient dusts with workplace exposure limits $> 1 	ext{ mg/m}^3$. Minimum filtration efficiency rating of $99\%$.
* **M (*Medium Risk*):** Mandatory legally certified specification for processing quartz, concrete dust, hardwood shavings, mineral dusts, and paint over-sprays with exposure limits $\ge 0.1 	ext{ mg/m}^3$. Minimum filtration efficiency rating of $99.9\%$.
* **H (*High Risk*):** Critically certified safety cleaner designed to isolate high-risk, carcinogenic, pathogenic, and toxic particulate matter such as airborne asbestos fibers, lead paint residue, chemical dust, mold spores, and heavy micro-pathogens with exposure limits $< 0.1 	ext{ mg/m}^3$. Minimum filtration efficiency rating of $99.995\%$.

### Hardware Configuration Details
* **Me:** *Metallic Container*. Built with a high-grade stainless steel debris tank/chassis shell instead of standard impact-resistant polypropylene plastic.
* **Bs:** *Bäckersauger* (Bakery Special). Specialized variant outfitted with high-temperature-resistant compound hoses, thermal safety filters, and dedicated anti-static accessories explicitly engineered to vacuum flour dust hazards and hot interior baking ovens.
* **Mwf:** *Mobile Wasserförderung Feuerwehr* (Mobile Flood Extraction for Fire Brigades). Built explicitly for high-rate disaster response, flood mitigation, and emergency pumping. The unit features an internal, high-flow submersible wastewater discharge pump anchored to the container bottom and specialized industrial Geka fluid couplings to continuously pump out extracted liquids simultaneously while suctioning.
* **K:** *Kipper / Pumpe*. Indicates either a convenient tilting/dumping articulated chassis frame layout or the inclusion of an internal fluid transfer pump.
* **C / M / S (Product Classes):** Separates machines into physical frame and performance classes: **C** = Compact Class (maneuverable, light storage footprint); **M** = Middle Class (balanced all-around durability); **S** = Super Class (maximum continuous power output, highest flow rates, maximum daily operational lifetime).
* **W:** *Walk-Behind*. Describes floor scrubbers or sweeping machines propelled or assisted via a built-in motorized traction wheel drive system operated by a walking user.
* **R:** *Ride-On*. Specifies that the machine is operated by a driver seated directly on an integrated machine operator platform.
* **Classic vs. Adv:** **Classic** lines denote cost-effective, entry-level functional iterations concentrating strictly on heavy mechanical performance with minimal electronic extras. **Adv** (*Advanced*) marks the premium spec line incorporating electronic warning arrays, upgraded hose specifications, and top-tier accessories bundled standard.
* **Bp:** *Battery Powered*. Identifies standard cordless, emissions-free machinery driven by high-output internal DC lithium-ion battery blocks.
* **X / MX:** High-pressure washer frames featuring an integrated, structural manual or spring-loaded high-pressure hose reel mechanism built directly into the frame molding.

## 5. Detergents & Chemicals: The "RM" System

Every proprietary cleaning agent, powder, liquid chemical concentrate, or specialty maintenance wax manufactured by Kärcher is designated globally by an **RM** prefix (*Reinigungsmittel* - Cleaning Agent) followed by a 2 or 3-digit numerical index.

| RM Number Code | Primary Workspace Classification | Focus Core & Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RM 10 – RM 100** | Heavy Industrial Maintenance & System Care | Heavy-duty chemical descalers, anti-corrosive boiler coil protectors for HDS machines, and highly alkaline engine block degreasers. |
| **RM 500 – RM 699** | Consumer Retail Line ("Home & Garden") | Pre-diluted, consumer-facing car shampoos, domestic wood patio cleaners, and retail household glass sprays. |
| **RM 700 – RM 799** | Commercial Floor Care, Carpets & Textiles | Low-foaming surfactant formulas optimized specifically for automated scrubber-driers, non-resolving carpet extraction powders, and chemical fabric floor wax strippers. |
| **RM 800 – RM 899** | High-Pressure Commercial Wash & Vehicle Bays | High-foaming touchless truck washes, agricultural bio-security disinfectants, and active chemical foam lances for fleet washing. |

### Technical Chemical Modifiers
* **ASF (*Auslesefreundlich*):** Separation Friendly. This chemical configuration ensures that when the dirty wastewater collects in a maintenance garage drain basin, the oily grease molecules rapidly break cleanly away from the water molecules. This allows commercial oil/water separator interceptor tanks to function properly, ensuring legal environmental discharge compliance.
* **Tabs:** High-density, individually wrapped, water-soluble dry compressed chemical tablets. Designed for pre-measured, exact dosing inside Puzzi carpet extraction machines, preventing chemical overdosing and minimizing shipping weight.
* **Eco!efficiency:** Chemical solutions engineered using highly biodegradable, sustainably sourced raw surfactants, completely free of harsh nitrilotriacetic acid (NTA) compounds.

## 6. Professional High-Pressure Accessories & Connections

### The EASY! Universe Transition
To eliminate the down-time and thread damage typical of traditional coarse metric thread designs, Kärcher transitioned all professional high-pressure machinery (HD/HDS) to the proprietary **EASY!** connection ecosystem. This completely separates professional accessories from consumer accessories.

```
[Machine Pressure Output] ──(Hose)──> [EASY!Force Gun] ──(Lance)──> [Power Nozzle]
```

* **EASY!Force Trigger Gun:** Utilizes the physical recoil force of the high-pressure water stream pushing against the valve body to hold the yellow activation trigger down into the palm of the operator automatically. This eliminates continuous grip strain, preventing hand fatigue during extended operation.
* **EASY!Lock Connection:** A multi-start, high-precision trapezoidal thread form. It provides the robust structural seal and mechanical load integrity of a traditional threaded metal nut, but requires only a short **$360^\circ$ single rotation** to lock or unlock hoses, lances, and spray nozzles.

### The 8-Piece Technical Adapter System
Because older fleets utilize legacy M22 metric screw threads, Kärcher utilizes a numbered 1-to-8 adapter matrix to bridge standard connections safely without compromising pressure ratings:
* **Adapters 1 through 4:** Engineered exclusively for converting components along the high-pressure **Hose** pathway (converting old M22 threads over to the new EASY!Lock trapezoidal structure).
* **Adapters 5 through 8:** Configured exclusively to adapt hardware downstream of the gun along the **Lance and Nozzle** delivery pathway (e.g., connecting standard metric spray tips or specialty rotating dirt blaster lances to an EASY!Force gun).

### High-Pressure Nozzle Coding Rules
Nozzles are stamped with a 5-digit technical geometry code that describes their spray behavior:
* *Example:* **Power Nozzle Code 25045**
    * **The First Two Digits (25):** The flat fan spray angle in degrees ($25^\circ$ provides an optimal industrial balance between sweeping surface area and high structural impact velocity; $0^\circ$ is a solid pencil jet; $40^\circ$ is a wide, lower-impact wash fan).
    * **The Last Three Digits (045):** The absolute physical internal nozzle orifice size index ($45$). This number must be matched precisely to the internal pump engineering parameters (flow rate vs. operating pressure).
    * *Catalog Rule:* If a nozzle with an internal size index that is *too small* is attached, the machine will suffer over-pressurization, causing severe surging or blown bypass safety valves. If a size that is *too large* is selected, water will flow freely but the machine will experience a severe drop in operational washing pressure.

## 7. Floor Care Consumables & Filtration Standards

### Mechanical Roller Brushes (BR System)
Cylindrical roller brushes are strictly color-coded by raw material composition and physical bristle diameter:
* **White:** Ultra-soft bristles. Optimized for cleaning delicate carpets, high-end stone, or highly sensitive decorative tile installations.
* **Red:** Standard medium stiffness. The all-around commercial baseline brush for daily washing on standard hard floors.
* **Green:** Coarse abrasive bristles. Impregnated with silicone carbide grit for deep scrubbing, stripping heavy dirt, and cleaning unpolished exterior industrial concrete.
* **Black:** Heavy-duty carbon-infused stripping bristles. Designed exclusively to aggressively remove tough floor coatings, old wax layers, and heavy chemical residue.

### Flat Disc Pads (BD System)
Flat round cleaning pads adhere to an industry-standard abrasive density grading color profile:
* **White / Beige:** Non-abrasive, loose weave. Tailored for dry high-speed buffing and fine wax polishing.
* **Red:** Light density. Engineered for everyday commercial washing and spray-cleaning maintenance.
* **Green:** Medium-high grit density. Used for aggressive deep scrubbing to lift embedded scuff marks and deep dirt.
* **Black:** Maximum abrasive density. Impregnated with tough grit for full chemical floor stripping down to raw structural concrete or stone substrates.

### Filtration Elements & Dust Collection Bags
* **Fleece Bags vs. Paper Bags:** Industrial professional vacuums rely on heavy-duty **3-layer high-tensile fleece bags**. Unlike standard cheap paper consumer bags, fleece bags stretch without bursting when processing sharp objects like screws, wood shavings, or stone grit, maintaining high airflow even when full.
* **PTFE Coated Filters:** Flat pleated filters lined with a non-stick polytetrafluoroethylene (Teflon) membrane. They are highly hydrophobic, allowing seamless switching from liquid to fine sticky dust particles without blinding the filter material.

## 8. The Battery Universe Architecture

Kärcher splits its cordless battery platforms into two isolated, non-interchangeable infrastructure systems based on workplace demands.

### Consumer Platform: "Battery Power"
* **Voltages:** Available in **18 V** and **36 V** configurations.
* **Chassis ID:** Plainly stamped with **Battery Power** branding.
* **Technology:** Features an integrated LCD display utilizing *Real Time Technology*. This micro-chip communication displays the exact remaining runtime down to the single minute, the remaining charging time required, and the exact state-of-charge percentage, rather than standard generic LED battery bars.

### Professional Platform: "Battery Power+"
* **Voltages:** Available in high-capacity **18 V** and **36 V** industrial blocks.
* **The "+" Sign Distinction:** Stamped clearly with the **"+"** modifier.
* **Engineering Architecture:** Built inside heavy-duty, impact-resistant, fire-retardant polyamide housings featuring laser-welded internal structural cells and robust copper cell busbars. This specialized low-resistance construction allows the battery to deliver high continuous current draws safely without thermal throttling or shutdown.
* **Catalog Safety Warning:** Professional "Battery Power+" packs use a slightly altered physical keyway slot rail. They cannot be forced into standard consumer-line battery tool docks, preventing accidental component damage from high-draw professional battery configurations.

## 9. The Universal 8-Digit Part Number Matrix (`X.XXX-XXX.0`)

Every component manufactured by Kärcher—from a massive city street sweeper to an individual rubber O-ring seal—is tracked by an 8-digit part index formatted strictly as `X.XXX-XXX.0`. The absolute first digit tells you exactly what functional category the product belongs to:

* **`1.XXX-XXX.0` ➔ Finished Machinery Units:** Represents a complete, fully operational boxed machine out of the factory (e.g., a complete HD washer system, a Puzzi extractor, or an NT vacuum).
* **`2.XXX-XXX.0` ➔ Operational Accessories:** Add-on components that modify or extend what a finished machine can do (e.g., separate surface cleaners, longer hose extensions, or specialty telescoping washing lances).
* **`4.XXX-XXX.0` ➔ Spare Part Assemblies:** Mechanical repair components or pre-assembled replacement modules intended for maintenance technicians (e.g., an assembled replacement brass pump cylinder head or a structural electric motor casing assembly).
* **`5.XXX-XXX.0` ➔ Individual Molded Parts:** Specific internal hardware, custom gears, specialized gaskets, and individually stamped plastic or metallic frame elements.
* **`6.XXX-XXX.0` ➔ Core Consumables & Chemistry:** High-volume wear components and consumables. This covers all replacement filters, fleece vacuum collection bags, and all bottled liquid, powder, or tablet detergents.

## 10. Special Industrial Application Platforms

### IB Line (*Ice Blaster*)
* **Application:** Cryogenic dry-ice blasting machines used to clean delicate electrical circuit boards, aerospace components, or heavy automotive molds without using water, chemicals, or abrasive media.
* **Code Pattern:** `IB [Hopper Capacity in kg] / [Maximum Air Blast Pressure in Bar]`.
* *Example:* **IB 15/120** = Dry-ice blaster holding a 15 kg pellet hopper, capable of handling an air blast pressure up to 120 bar.
* **L2P (*Liquid-to-Pellet*):** A highly specialized modifier designation indicating that the machine converts liquid $CO_2$ directly into solid dry ice pellets internally on demand, eliminating the logistical challenges of purchasing and storing pre-made dry ice blocks.

### FR & FRV Flat Surface Cleaners
* **FR Platform:** Flat surface round rotary decks containing multiple high-pressure spinning nozzles. They hover over large flat floors or driveways, washing surfaces up to 10 times faster than a standard single lance without leaving zebra stripe marks.
* **FRV Platform (*Flat Surface Cleaner Vacuum*):** The upgraded version featuring an integrated internal venturi fluid extraction channel. As the nozzles blast the floor, the kinetic water energy creates a suction vacuum that automatically draws the dirty wastewater up and discharges it out through an attached 5-meter drainage hose, leaving the interior warehouse floor completely dry.

### MC / MIC Platforms
* **Application:** Municipal Equipment / Multi-functional Industrial Utility Implement Carriers. These are actual road-registered municipal vehicles configured for municipal street sweeping, park maintenance, or winter snow plowing.
* *Example:* **MC 130** = Municipal Compact utility vehicle with a 1.3 cubic meter waste container capacity.
